What goes wrong

Limits to know

  • The model always picks one of 10 intents, even for text outside them.
  • Scores are uncalibrated and the model is often unsure: correct test answers averaged about 33% confidence, wrong ones about 19%.
  • Only 50 test rows (5 per intent), so a single error moves an intent's F1 by 0.1 or more.
  • Character n-gram model. It does not understand grammar or meaning.
